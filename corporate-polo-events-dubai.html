FAQ

Common Questions

Do we need any experience to join?

No, lessons are designed for complete beginners and are led safely from the ground up.

How many people can join a corporate lesson?

Group sizes are flexible — share your headcount and Niels will tailor the format to suit your group.

Can this be combined with a private event or hospitality package?

Yes, get in touch to discuss combining polo with broader event or hospitality arrangements at Desert Palm Polo Club.

What does a corporate lesson cost?

Corporate lessons start from AED 1,000 + VAT per person for a 2-hour lesson, minimum 10 guests. Get in touch for a tailored quote based on your group size and format.

Is this safe for complete beginners?

Yes. Lessons are held at Desert Palm Polo Club, which carries venue insurance, and all participants sign a standard liability disclaimer before taking part. Polo carries inherent risk like any equestrian sport, but lessons are led safely from the ground up for complete beginners.
