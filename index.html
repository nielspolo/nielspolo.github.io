FAQ

Common Questions

How much are polo lessons in Dubai?

Private lessons start from AED 800 + VAT per lesson — fully tailored 1-on-1 coaching with a +3 handicap coach who has trained 200+ students. Corporate and group lessons start from AED 1,000 + VAT per person for a 2-hour lesson (minimum 10 guests). Get in touch for an exact quote based on your goals.

Do I need experience to start playing polo?

No. The Beginner Foundations program is built for complete beginners, covering grip, stance, and horse control from your very first lesson.
