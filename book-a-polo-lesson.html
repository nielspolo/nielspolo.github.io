What Happens Next. Submit the form or reach out directly, and Niels will personally review your goals and experience before responding — no automated booking system, no call centre. Most enquiries hear back within 24 hours with a suggested program and available times.

If you're not sure which program fits, mention that in your message. Complete beginners typically start with Beginner Foundations; returning riders and those with other equestrian experience often move straight into Stick & Ball Clinics or Private 1-on-1 Coaching. Corporate and group bookings are scheduled separately to accommodate larger parties.

Lessons take place at Dubai Polo & Equestrian Club, Dubai — full directions are shared once your booking is confirmed.
