Why Private Coaching

The Fastest Path to Real Improvement

Private polo coaching in Dubai is a fully personalised, 1-on-1 lesson with Niels van Baasbank at Desert Palm Polo Club, with every minute spent entirely on your mount, your swing, your positioning, and your mindset. Niels holds a career-high +3 handicap, brings 30 years of international experience, and has trained 200+ students, adjusting his teaching style as your game evolves.

Whether you're a complete beginner or a competitive player chasing a handicap increase, lessons are structured around measurable improvement, not generic drills.

Mallet technique and swing mechanics
Horse control and communication
Positional play and game strategy
Mental preparation for competition
Personalised feedback between lessons

Who This Is For

Built Around Your Goals

Private coaching is built for riders who are serious about progressing quickly: players preparing for tournaments, and anyone who wants a program built entirely around their own weaknesses and schedule rather than a fixed class structure.

FAQ

Common Questions

How much does private polo coaching cost in Dubai?

Private lessons start from AED 800 + VAT per lesson for fully tailored 1-on-1 coaching with a +3 handicap coach. Get in touch for an exact quote based on your goals and schedule.

How long is a private lesson?

Most private lessons run 45–60 minutes. Exact length is flexible and confirmed with Niels when you book, based on your goals and schedule.

How often should I train privately?

Most students train one to two times per week. Niels recommends a schedule based on your level and goals.

Do I need experience to start private lessons?

No. Private coaching works for complete beginners through competitive tournament players, since every lesson is tailored to your current level from the very first session.

Can I combine private lessons with group clinics?

Yes, many students combine private 1-on-1 lessons with Stick & Ball Clinics or Team Training for a well-rounded program.
