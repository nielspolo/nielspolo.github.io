Watch & Learn with Niels

These clips are pulled straight from real coaching sessions at Dubai Polo & Equestrian Club — no scripts, just the technique corrections Niels makes most often with new and returning students. Each one focuses on a single fix you can take onto the field immediately, whether you're practising alone or booking your next lesson.

Foot Mallet

Offside Practice

A foot mallet drill Niels uses to sharpen offside timing without a horse underneath you. It isolates the swing mechanics that most beginners rush, so the correction sticks once you're back in the saddle. Run through it a few times before your next lesson and Niels will build directly on the habit.

Most Common Mistakes

Coaching Insight

The errors Niels sees most often in first-time and intermediate riders, and the coaching cues he uses to correct them. If something in this video looks familiar, it's worth flagging in your next session so he can watch for it specifically.

Mallet Position

Practice Advice

Correct mallet position matters more than raw swing power in polo, and it's usually the first thing Niels checks with a new student. This short breakdown shows the grip and wrist angle he coaches from lesson one through advanced play.
