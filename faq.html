FAQ

Common Questions

How much are polo lessons in Dubai?

Private lessons start from AED 800 + VAT per lesson — fully tailored 1-on-1 coaching with a +3 handicap coach who has trained 200+ students.

Do I need experience to start playing polo?

No. The Beginner Foundations program is built for complete beginners, covering grip, stance, and horse control from your very first lesson.

Where can I learn polo in Dubai?

Niels coaches at Dubai Polo & Equestrian Club, Dubai — offering private 1-on-1 lessons, stick & ball clinics, and tournament preparation for all levels. All lessons are booked directly with Niels, not through the club.

What is a polo handicap?

A polo handicap rates a player's skill from -2 (beginner) up to +10 (elite professional). Niels holds a career-high handicap of +3 and coaches players at every stage of that scale.

Do you offer corporate polo experiences?

Yes. Niels runs corporate and social polo lessons for groups — a safe, professional introduction to the sport of kings, no experience required. Corporate and group lessons start from AED 1,000 + VAT per person for a 2-hour lesson (minimum 10 guests). Get in touch for an exact quote based on your goals.

What programs does Niels offer?

Six structured programs to match every stage: Beginner Foundations, Stick & Ball Clinics, Tournament Preparation, Private 1-on-1 Coaching, Team Training, and Corporate & Social Polo — each led personally by Niels at Dubai Polo & Equestrian Club.

What are Niels' coaching qualifications?

Niels holds an HPA Level 2 coaching certification from the Hurlingham Polo Association, is HPA-registered as an Overseas Polo Coach, and has coached 200+ students over more than 30 years in the sport, including two decades based in Dubai.

What times are lessons available?

Lessons run seven days a week, 6am to 7pm, so you can build a schedule around work, weather, or the cooler early-morning and evening slots many students prefer in Dubai.
