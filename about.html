The Journey

Thirty Years in the Saddle

Niels van Baasbank is a Dutch equestrian polo coach with over 30 years of international coaching and club management experience, including 20 years based in Dubai. He currently serves as Head Coach at Desert Palm Polo Club, bringing a rare combination of playing excellence and coaching expertise to every lesson.

His career began with founding the first polo academy in the Netherlands, before he moved to the UAE to manage Emaar's flagship Dubai Polo & Equestrian Club. Along the way he refined his own game through years of study in Argentine polo technique, and has since coached hundreds of students ranging from complete beginners to competitive tournament players.

Today, Niels combines this playing pedigree with a coaching style built on patience, structure, and genuinely tailored feedback — an approach that has made him one of the most trusted names in polo coaching in the UAE.

"Polo is not just a sport — it is a conversation between horse and rider, strategy and instinct, discipline and freedom."

Coaching Philosophy

Structure, Patience, and Real Feedback

Every student learns differently, so Niels builds each program around the individual rather than a fixed curriculum. Lessons are structured to give measurable progress, whether that means fixing a swing that has been wrong for years or preparing a team for its next tournament.

Beyond technique, Niels places equal weight on horsemanship and mental preparation, believing that a well-rounded player understands their horse as deeply as they understand the game itself.
